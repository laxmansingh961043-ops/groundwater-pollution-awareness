Toxic aquifers are poisoning millions — a silent health crisis beneath our feet.

The invisible threat

Industrial runoff, agricultural chemicals, and untreated sewage seep into underground reserves. Once contaminated, aquifers remain polluted for decades — exposing millions to arsenic, nitrates, and heavy metals.

Industrial waste: 70% of heavy metal pollution.
Fertilizers & pesticides: nitrate contamination.
Leaking septic systems: bacterial pathogens.
